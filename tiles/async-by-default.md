We work in writing. Each week you get a short update on what shipped, what comes next, and what we need from you. Questions go in a shared thread, so nobody waits for the next meeting to get an answer. Your team stays focused on its own work and still knows where the project stands. We still meet when a conversation is the fastest way forward.
